Watchlist protocols, ranked by implied value

The watchlist is the floor: every protocol with at least $50M of TVL that records no token. The early radar adds pre-token protocols below that floor, by TVL. Implied value is the snapshot's own market speaking — TVL times the mcap/TVL ratio of tokenized peers in the same category — not a promise of a launch.

A protocol records no token when the snapshot carries no market cap, CoinGecko id or symbol for it. That is a record, not proof it has none.

How implied value is computed

Four named functions score every row from the snapshot's own figures. No protocol name is special-cased, and no row is hand-picked.

implied = round( tvl_usd × comparableRatio(category) × growthMultiplier(change_7d) × ageMultiplier(age_days) × chainMultiplier(chains) )

  • comparableRatio — the median mcap/TVL of tokenized protocols in the same category, shrunk toward the global median when the category has few peers, clamped to 0.02–2.50.
  • growthMultiplier — a damped log response to seven-day TVL growth (clamped −60% to +200%): a hot week lifts value, but one hot week can never dominate.
  • ageMultiplier — pent-up value: longer listed with no token on record means more value waiting, rising from 0.75 toward 1.20 and never past it.
  • chainMultiplier — a 3% premium per extra chain, up to four, with a small penalty when the snapshot names no category.
